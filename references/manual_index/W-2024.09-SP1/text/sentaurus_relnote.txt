   TCAD SentaurusTM Release Notes
                on SolvNetPlus

To access the TCAD Sentaurus release notes on the Synopsys® SolvNetPlus support site:
1. Go to https://solvnetplus.synopsys.com.
2. Enter your user name and password.

   If you do not have a Synopsys user name and password, then click REGISTER - CREATE
   ACCOUNT to register with SolvNetPlus.
3. Click Sign In.
   The home page of SolvNetPlus opens.
4. Click Downloads.
5. On the My Product Releases tab, scroll through the list and click TCAD Sentaurus.
6. Click the software version you require.
7. Click the View release notes link.
